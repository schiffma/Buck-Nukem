# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Buck's Thurgau kill lines
In every level, Buck SHALL comment on kills with short Thurgau-dialect lines in the tone of a cocky, flirty agglo macho, never explicit, with the quote packs' cooldown and placement. On foot, each enemy type SHALL have its own set of at least 12 lines. On the ride, the Thurgau lines SHALL join the existing car-kill lines. The lines SHALL be original, not quoted from a TV show.

#### Scenario: Kill on foot
- **WHEN** Buck finishes off a lawyer on the clay courts and has not said a kill line in the last 3 seconds
- **THEN** a Thurgau line from the lawyer set (for example "PLÄDOYER ABGLEHNT.") appears in a bubble above his head

#### Scenario: Every level on foot
- **WHEN** Buck kills a lawyer, crayfish or gardener-punk in the courts, creek, clubhouse or boss arena (including a crayfish the boss summoned)
- **THEN** he can say a line, following the same 3-second cooldown as on the ride

#### Scenario: Ride keeps its lines
- **WHEN** Buck destroys a car on the ride
- **THEN** the line comes from the ride's original lines or the added Thurgau car lines, and the limo finale still uses its own lines

#### Scenario: Content checks
- **WHEN** the game is built and tested
- **THEN** every new line is at most 20 characters, uses only font glyphs, appears at most once in its section, and each on-foot enemy set has at least 12 lines
