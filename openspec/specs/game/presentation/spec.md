# game/presentation Specification

## Purpose

Defines how the game looks and sounds: the fixed 320x240 retro display, the hero and photo-based backgrounds, the HUD and screens, and the Ride of the Valkyries soundtrack and sound effects.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Native 320x240 resolution
The game SHALL render every frame at an internal resolution of exactly 320×240 pixels and scale it to the largest integer multiple that fits the browser window (minimum 1×), centred on a black background, with nearest-neighbour scaling and no smoothing.

#### Scenario: Window resize
- **WHEN** the browser window is resized to 1280×1000
- **THEN** the game is shown at 4× (1280×960), centred, with sharp pixels

#### Scenario: Small window
- **WHEN** the window is smaller than 640×480
- **THEN** the game is shown at 1×

### Requirement: Hero appearance
The hero SHALL be a pixel-art character about 32 pixels tall with natural proportions, a small head and a slight belly, whose face is pixelled with colours sampled from `assets/img/buck_nukem.jpg`, wearing a dark jacket, a white shirt over the belly and black clogs and carrying a large gun. Sprites SHALL use dark 1-px outlines and 3-tone shading. The hero SHALL have distinct idle, run, jump, climb, shoot and hurt poses, mirrored when facing left.

#### Scenario: Facing direction
- **WHEN** the player moves left
- **THEN** the hero sprite is drawn mirrored to face left

#### Scenario: Outlined sprites
- **WHEN** any hero or enemy sprite is drawn over a background
- **THEN** every opaque edge pixel of the sprite borders a dark outline pixel

#### Scenario: Head proportion
- **WHEN** any hero frame is built
- **THEN** the head occupies at most 9 of the 32 pixel rows (about a quarter), and the body (torso and legs) occupies at least 23 rows

#### Scenario: Belly
- **WHEN** the hero is drawn standing
- **THEN** the white shirt over the belly bulges forward so the torso is 1 to 2 pixels wider at belly height than at the shoulders

#### Scenario: Face colours from the photo
- **WHEN** the hero head is generated by the build
- **THEN** its hair, skin, lens and moustache pixels use colours sampled from the corresponding regions of `assets/img/buck_nukem.jpg`, and the long grey-blond hair reaching the shoulders, the tinted sunglasses, the white horseshoe moustache, the stubble and the cigar are visible as separate features

#### Scenario: Clogs
- **WHEN** the hero is drawn in any pose with feet visible (idle, run, jump, shoot, hurt, climb)
- **THEN** each foot is a black clog (black upper and dark sole)

#### Scenario: Photo portrait unchanged
- **WHEN** the title, game-over or victory screen is shown
- **THEN** the 96x96 photo portrait is used as before

### Requirement: Photo-based backgrounds
Each level SHALL use one of the TC Wollerau or Krebsbach photos from `assets/img/` as a scrolling parallax background, colour-reduced to a retro palette, graded at build time to a lurid 1970s colour-print look (more saturated than the photo, warm cast, mild contrast, brightness close to the photo) and scrolling slower than the foreground.

#### Scenario: Parallax scrolling
- **WHEN** the camera scrolls 100 pixels horizontally
- **THEN** the background scrolls by fewer than 100 pixels

#### Scenario: Gritty grade
- **WHEN** a processed background is compared to the same crop of the source photo
- **THEN** its average saturation is higher, its average brightness is at least 90% of the photo's, and its colours are shifted toward warm tones

### Requirement: Themed foreground tiles
Foreground tiles SHALL match each level's theme, drawn worn and dirty (cracks, stains, rust, grime): clay-court surfaces, nets, fences and tennis equipment for TC Wollerau; grass, gravel and toxic red water for the Krebsbach creek; a furnished clubhouse interior (see "Clubhouse interior") followed by walls and floors of the indoor hall for level 3.

#### Scenario: Creek water
- **WHEN** the Krebsbach level is shown
- **THEN** creek water tiles are visible, toxic red and animated

#### Scenario: Worn tile variants
- **WHEN** a row of identical solid tiles is drawn
- **THEN** at least two visibly different worn variants of the tile are used so the surface does not repeat uniformly

### Requirement: HUD
During play a HUD SHALL show score, health as a segmented bar, lives, the current weapon and collected key cards, using a pixel font, without hiding the hero.

#### Scenario: Health display updates
- **WHEN** the hero loses one health point
- **THEN** the health bar shows one segment fewer in the same frame

### Requirement: Title and end screens
The game SHALL show a title screen styled as a 1970s exploitation-movie poster: the game name "BUCK NUKEM" in large distressed, outlined lettering, a tagline, a fake rating badge, the pixelated portrait from the hero photo, a controls summary, "PRESS ENTER" and the build version in small print. It SHALL also have level-complete, game-over and victory screens; game over and victory SHALL be styled as "THE END" film cards.

#### Scenario: Starting the game
- **WHEN** the player presses Enter on the title screen
- **THEN** level 1 starts with score 0, full health and 3 lives

#### Scenario: Controls summary
- **WHEN** the title screen is shown
- **THEN** the controls list shows Space for jump, X/Ctrl/Alt for fire and Up/Down for climbing and kneeling

#### Scenario: Version shown
- **WHEN** the title screen is shown
- **THEN** the version text embedded by the build (for example "V1.0.2" or "V1.0.2-3-GAB12CD3") is drawn in small print in a corner of the poster, fully inside the screen, readable on the poster tint and not overlapping the other title elements

### Requirement: Ride of the Valkyries soundtrack
The game SHALL play a looping chiptune arrangement of Wagner's "Ride of the Valkyries", synthesized in the browser, during play. Because browsers block audio until the user interacts, music SHALL start on the first key press.

#### Scenario: Music starts after first input
- **WHEN** the player presses any key on the title screen
- **THEN** the Ride of the Valkyries theme starts playing and loops

#### Scenario: Boss music intensifies
- **WHEN** the boss arena starts
- **THEN** the theme plays at a faster tempo

### Requirement: Sound effects
The game SHALL play synthesized sound effects for shooting, jumping, enemy hits, explosions, pickups, taking damage, doors and the boss's claw slam.

#### Scenario: Shot sound
- **WHEN** the hero fires
- **THEN** a short shot sound plays

### Requirement: Mute toggle
Pressing M SHALL toggle all music and sound effects on and off.

#### Scenario: Muting
- **WHEN** the player presses M while audio is on
- **THEN** all music and sound effects stop until M is pressed again

### Requirement: Film-grain post-processing
Every rendered 320×240 frame SHALL pass through a film filter that applies a warm, saturated colour-print grade, animated grain, occasional vertical scratches, dust specks, a light vignette, a 0–1 pixel frame jitter and slight brightness flicker. The grade SHALL NOT desaturate the image. The filter SHALL keep update and render together under 8 ms per frame on a desktop browser.

#### Scenario: Filter visible
- **WHEN** two consecutive frames of a static scene are rendered with the filter on
- **THEN** the frames differ (grain/flicker animate) and the screen corners are darker than the centre

#### Scenario: Grade keeps colour
- **WHEN** a uniform saturated colour frame (for example pure clay-court orange) is passed through the filter
- **THEN** the output's average chroma is at least as high as the input's

#### Scenario: Light vignette
- **WHEN** a uniform mid-grey frame is passed through the filter
- **THEN** the corners are darker than the centre but at least 70% as bright

#### Scenario: Performance budget
- **WHEN** 300 frames of level 1 are updated and rendered with the filter on
- **THEN** the average time per frame is under 8 ms

### Requirement: Film filter toggle
Pressing G SHALL toggle the film filter on and off at any time. The filter SHALL be on when the game starts.

#### Scenario: Toggling the filter
- **WHEN** the player presses G while the filter is on
- **THEN** subsequent frames are rendered without grade, grain, scratches, vignette and jitter until G is pressed again

### Requirement: Damage flash
When the hero takes damage, the screen edges SHALL flash red for about a quarter of a second.

#### Scenario: Hero hurt
- **WHEN** the hero loses health
- **THEN** a red vignette overlay appears and fades out within about 0.25 seconds

### Requirement: Reel title cards
Each level intro SHALL be shown as a film-reel title card: a short countdown leader (3, 2, 1) followed by "REEL n" and the level name. Before the boss arena, a "MISSING REEL" gag card SHALL be shown briefly. Pressing Enter or Space SHALL skip the card.

#### Scenario: Level intro
- **WHEN** level 2 starts
- **THEN** a countdown leader is shown, then "REEL 2" with the level name, then play begins

#### Scenario: Missing reel before the boss
- **WHEN** the hero exits level 3
- **THEN** a "MISSING REEL" card is shown before the boss arena's intro card

### Requirement: Clubhouse interior
The clubhouse part of level 3 SHALL be drawn as the furnished interior of the TC Wollerau clubhouse. It SHALL have a wall backdrop of wood panelling and wallpaper instead of the photo, a bar area and a restaurant area, plus club decoration: trophy cabinet, framed photos or posters, tennis rackets on the wall and hanging lamps.

#### Scenario: Interior backdrop
- **WHEN** the camera shows the clubhouse part of level 3
- **THEN** empty space inside the clubhouse is drawn as panelled wall and wallpaper, and the aerial photo is not visible there

#### Scenario: Photo outside the clubhouse
- **WHEN** the camera shows the indoor hall part of level 3
- **THEN** the graded photo backdrop is used as in the other levels

### Requirement: Clubhouse bar
The clubhouse SHALL contain a bar with a counter, bar stools, beer taps, a back-bar shelf of bottles, a neon beer sign and a cash register.

#### Scenario: Bar visible
- **WHEN** the hero walks through the bar area of level 3
- **THEN** the counter, stools, taps, bottle shelf and neon sign are visible, and the neon sign flickers

### Requirement: Clubhouse restaurant
The clubhouse SHALL contain a restaurant with tables with checked tablecloths, chairs, a menu chalkboard listing CERVELAT, RIVELLA and FONDUE with prices, a kitchen pass-through and potted plants.

#### Scenario: Restaurant visible
- **WHEN** the hero walks through the restaurant area of level 3
- **THEN** tables with chairs, the menu chalkboard and the kitchen pass-through are visible

#### Scenario: Menu board text
- **WHEN** the menu chalkboard is drawn
- **THEN** it shows the lines "CERVELAT", "RIVELLA" and "FONDUE" with prices in the pixel font

### Requirement: Toxic Krebsbach creek
The creek water in the Krebsbach level SHALL look toxic: a deep red liquid with glowing red-orange highlights, popping bubbles and a faint red glow above the surface. Splashes and the bubbles of hidden crayfish SHALL be red. The level SHALL show toxic waste drums and skull "TOXIC" warning signs near the pools. Water damage and bounce SHALL stay unchanged.

#### Scenario: Red water
- **WHEN** a creek water tile is drawn
- **THEN** its dominant colour is red (red channel clearly above green and blue) and no blue water colour remains

#### Scenario: Bubbles animate
- **WHEN** two frames of the creek water are rendered a quarter of a second apart
- **THEN** the bubble pixels on the surface differ between the frames

#### Scenario: Toxic splash
- **WHEN** the hero touches the creek water
- **THEN** the splash particles are red, and the hero loses 2 health and bounces as before

#### Scenario: Warning signs and drums
- **WHEN** the Krebsbach level is loaded
- **THEN** it contains at least two toxic waste drums and at least two skull warning signs reading "TOXIC"

### Requirement: Tennis court equipment
The TC Wollerau clay-court level SHALL be furnished with tennis equipment drawn as background decoration: an umpire chair, a ball machine, ball baskets, racket bags, player benches, a scoreboard showing "TC WOLLERAU" and a score, court drag brushes and a water cooler. The equipment SHALL NOT block movement, and the level SHALL remain completable.

#### Scenario: Equipment present
- **WHEN** the clay-court level is loaded
- **THEN** every listed equipment type appears at least once in the level

#### Scenario: Equipment does not block
- **WHEN** the hero walks along the ground past the equipment
- **THEN** none of it blocks horizontal movement

#### Scenario: Level 1 completable
- **WHEN** the level reachability check runs on the furnished level 1
- **THEN** the exit, the key and all pickups are reachable from the start

### Requirement: Bright, colourful grindhouse look
With the film filter on, the title screen and every level SHALL look bright and colourful while keeping the grindhouse film character. Measured on the fixed benchmark scenes of the test suite (title, level 1 courts, level 2 creek, level 3 clubhouse and hall, boss arena), each final frame SHALL reach a mean brightness of at least 70 of 255 and a mean chroma of at least 0.25, and no more than 3% of pixels SHALL be clipped to pure white.

#### Scenario: Bright enough
- **WHEN** any benchmark scene is rendered with the film filter on
- **THEN** its mean luma (0.299 R + 0.587 G + 0.114 B) is at least 70

#### Scenario: Colourful enough
- **WHEN** any benchmark scene is rendered with the film filter on
- **THEN** its mean chroma ((max − min of R, G, B) / 255) is at least 0.25

#### Scenario: No blown-out highlights
- **WHEN** any benchmark scene is rendered with the film filter on
- **THEN** at most 3% of its pixels have all three channels at 250 or above

#### Scenario: Grindhouse character kept
- **WHEN** a benchmark scene is rendered over several frames with the film filter on
- **THEN** grain, flicker, scratches or dust still make consecutive frames differ, and the corners stay darker than the centre

### Requirement: Realistic red crayfish
The small crayfish and the giant boss SHALL look like the real crayfish in the change's reference photo, drawn side-on: red only (glossy red-brown armour, darker joints, pale claw tips; no blue), smooth carapace with a pointed rostrum, segmented tail with a tail fan, two large claws, thin legs and long antennae. Sprites keep dark 1-px outlines; hitboxes and behaviour stay unchanged; no glow effects.

#### Scenario: Red only
- **WHEN** a small crayfish frame or a boss frame is drawn
- **THEN** every opaque non-outline pixel is red-dominant or a near-neutral pale/dark tone (red channel at least as high as green and blue), and no pixel is blue-dominant (blue more than 20 above red)

#### Scenario: Crayfish anatomy
- **WHEN** a small crayfish sprite is built
- **THEN** it shows a segmented tail (at least three visible segment lines) ending in a tail fan, a claw at the front at least a quarter of the body length, thin legs below the body, and antennae extending forward at least half the body length

#### Scenario: Outlines kept
- **WHEN** any small crayfish frame is drawn over a background
- **THEN** every opaque edge pixel borders a dark outline pixel, as for all other sprites

#### Scenario: Gameplay unchanged
- **WHEN** a small crayfish or the boss is created
- **THEN** its hitboxes, health, speed and attack behaviour are the same as before the redesign

#### Scenario: No glow effects
- **WHEN** a small crayfish or the boss is on screen for one second
- **THEN** no glow particles are emitted and no helix or molecule glow is drawn around the boss

### Requirement: Kneeling pose
While kneeling, the hero SHALL be drawn in a kneeling pose: one knee on the ground, the other leg bent forward, the torso and head lowered accordingly, and the gun held level at knee height. The pose SHALL have a shooting variant with the muzzle flash at the lowered gun, and a disc-cutter variant in the boss arena. The pose SHALL keep the hero's palette, outline style and facing direction.

#### Scenario: Kneeling sprite
- **WHEN** the hero kneels facing right
- **THEN** the kneeling frame is drawn, its feet on the hero's floor and its top about one third lower than the standing frame's top

#### Scenario: Muzzle flash at knee height
- **WHEN** the kneeling hero fires the blaster
- **THEN** the muzzle flash appears at the lowered gun, at the height where the shot leaves

#### Scenario: Disc cutter while kneeling
- **WHEN** the hero kneels in the boss arena
- **THEN** he holds the disc cutter in the kneeling pose

### Requirement: Cheering tennis fans
Every level SHALL show a crowd of adult female tennis players in the background cheering for Buck: 8–10 in each regular level and 4–5 in the boss arena. Within a level each SHALL look different. They SHALL idle with a cheer animation, face Buck, and each SHALL talk to him in her own speech bubble with cheeky, flirty lines (suggestive innuendo, never explicit). They are purely decorative.

#### Scenario: Fans in every level
- **WHEN** any level is loaded
- **THEN** it contains 8 to 10 fans (4 to 5 in the boss arena), each standing on solid ground or a platform

#### Scenario: All different
- **WHEN** the fans of one level are compared
- **THEN** no two of them share the same combination of hair style, hair colour, skin tone, outfit accent and accessory, and the game has at least 14 distinct looks

#### Scenario: Idle cheer and facing Buck
- **WHEN** a fan is on screen
- **THEN** her sprite animates (at least two different frames within one second) and she faces the side Buck is on

#### Scenario: Reaction to Buck's success
- **WHEN** Buck kills an enemy, picks up a trophy or touches a checkpoint within 12 tiles of fans
- **THEN** every fan within that range plays a jump-and-wave cheer for about one second, the nearest ones show speech bubbles with lines for that occasion, and no two bubbles on screen show the same line

#### Scenario: Shout-outs when Buck walks past
- **WHEN** Buck comes within 4 tiles of a fan who has not called out to him in the last 8 seconds
- **THEN** she blows him a kiss, a small heart floats up from her, and she shows a flirty shout-out bubble (such as "HEY HANDSOME!")

#### Scenario: Flirty but not explicit
- **WHEN** the line pools are checked
- **THEN** there are at least 30 distinct lines across the occasions (shout-out, kill, trophy, checkpoint), each at most 20 characters and drawable with the game font (including a heart), and none contains explicit sexual words

#### Scenario: Readable bubbles
- **WHEN** several fans talk at the same time
- **THEN** at most 3 bubbles are shown, nearest fans first, they don't overlap each other, each fan shows at most one bubble at a time, and every bubble disappears after about 1.5 seconds

#### Scenario: Rate limit
- **WHEN** several reactions or shout-outs happen within a short time
- **THEN** the cheer sound plays at most once per 1.5 seconds and the wolf whistle at most once per 4 seconds

#### Scenario: No gameplay effect
- **WHEN** Buck, his shots or enemies pass a fan
- **THEN** nothing collides with her, nothing is blocked or damaged, and the level checker's results are unchanged

#### Scenario: Outlined pixel art
- **WHEN** any fan frame is drawn, including the blow-a-kiss frame
- **THEN** every opaque edge pixel borders a dark outline pixel, as for the other sprites

### Requirement: Cover crates look
Crates in the boss arena SHALL be drawn as outlined pixel-art wooden crates matching the creek theme. Sturdy crates SHALL look visibly reinforced (metal bands), so the player can tell them from breakable crates. A breakable crate SHALL show cracks as it takes damage and burst into wooden splinters with a crunch sound when it breaks.

#### Scenario: Two kinds distinguishable
- **WHEN** a sturdy and a breakable crate are drawn side by side
- **THEN** their tiles differ, and the sturdy one shows metal bands

#### Scenario: Damage and breaking visible
- **WHEN** a breakable crate has lost at least half its strength
- **THEN** it is drawn with cracks, and when it breaks splinter particles fly and a crate-break sound plays

### Requirement: Start reel note on the title
When the URL selects a start level other than level 1, the title screen SHALL show a small note naming it: "STARTS AT REEL N" for levels 2 and 3, "STARTS AT FINAL REEL" for the boss arena. Without a valid level parameter, or with `level=1`, no note SHALL be shown.

#### Scenario: Note shown
- **WHEN** the title screen is shown with `#level=3`
- **THEN** the text "STARTS AT REEL 3" is drawn in small print, fully inside the screen, readable on the poster tint and not overlapping the other title elements (including the version text)

#### Scenario: Boss arena note
- **WHEN** the title screen is shown with `#level=4`
- **THEN** the text "STARTS AT FINAL REEL" is drawn

#### Scenario: No note by default
- **WHEN** the title screen is shown without a level parameter, with `level=1` or with an invalid value
- **THEN** no start-reel note is drawn

### Requirement: Mega Cutter look
The Mega Cutter's pickup and blades SHALL look distinct from the plain disc cutter: the pickup is an outlined pixel-art disc cutter with a glowing orange blade, and its blades are larger (at least 16 pixels) with an orange glow, still spinning. The HUD weapon label SHALL read "MEGA CUTTER" while it is active.

#### Scenario: Distinct blades
- **WHEN** a plain cutter blade and a Mega Cutter blade are drawn
- **THEN** the Mega Cutter blade is at least 16 pixels wide, its frames differ from the plain blade's, and it contains orange glow pixels

#### Scenario: HUD label
- **WHEN** the hero has the Mega Cutter
- **THEN** the HUD shows "MEGA CUTTER" with the upgraded weapon icon

#### Scenario: Pickup readable
- **WHEN** the upgrade pickup is drawn on the top platform
- **THEN** it is outlined like the other pickups and differs from the Mega Blaster pickup
